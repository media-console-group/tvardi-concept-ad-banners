Biotech Investors Turn to Second-Half Clinical Readouts as Rate Worries Ease

By Business Desk Staff · Updated 2 hours ago · 4 min read

Shares of small- and mid-cap drug developers firmed this week as investors looked past macro headlines and toward a crowded calendar of mid-stage clinical readouts expected in the back half of the year.

Analysts say the shift reflects a broader rotation inside healthcare: after two years in which funding conditions dominated the sector's direction, investors are again underwriting individual programs, with oncology and fibrosis among the areas drawing the closest attention.
